**Program brand:** A1 Workshop  
**Format:** Self-paced open course (15 weeks; week 1 ready, later weeks stubbed)  
**Audience:** Advanced makers, engineers, entrepreneurs, researchers, and technical professionals  
**Prerequisites:** Basic computer literacy; no prior AI experience required

## Overview

AI Driven Invention teaches you to use modern AI systems as engineering collaborators throughout the product development lifecycle — not prompt engineering for its own sake.

You build practical **engineering harnesses** that connect large language models to professional tools (CAD, circuit simulation, source control, manufacturing, hardware platforms), held to **deterministic validation** and **human sign-off**.

A shared example product — a portable **environmental monitor** (temperature and humidity) — carries you from foundations through circuit, PCB, enclosure, software, and validation.

## Learning objectives

- Explain the difference between AI assistants, agents, and autonomous workflows.
- Design and build simple agentic engineering harnesses.
- Use AI to accelerate electronics design and documentation.
- Use AI-assisted CAD workflows for mechanical design.
- Integrate simulation tools into AI development loops.
- Generate and validate technical documentation.
- Build reproducible engineering workflows.
- Apply AI while maintaining engineering rigor and professional judgment.
- Complete a prototype invention using an AI-assisted product development pipeline.

## Capstone idea

Modern engineering is becoming:

```text
Engineer → Agentic Workflow → Engineering Tools → Validation → Product
```

not merely:

```text
Engineer → Tool → Product
```

The engineer remains responsible for judgment, creativity, safety, and validation.

**AI is not replacing inventors.**  
**AI is becoming one of the most powerful tools inventors have ever had.**
