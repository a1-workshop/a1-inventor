# Program Overview

The AI-Powered Inventor course, delivered under **A1 Workshop** in partnership with Humanmade, teaches students how to leverage modern AI systems as engineering collaborators throughout the product development lifecycle.

Rather than focusing on prompt engineering, the course introduces students to AI-assisted design, simulation, fabrication, software development, and documentation workflows used by modern engineers and technical innovators.

Students will learn how to build practical "engineering harnesses" that connect large language models to professional engineering tools including CAD software, circuit simulation environments, source control systems, manufacturing workflows, and hardware development platforms.

A shared example product—a portable **environmental monitor** (temperature and humidity)—carries students from foundations through circuit, PCB, enclosure, software, and validation.

The course culminates in a capstone project where participants use AI-assisted workflows to design, prototype, document, and present an original invention (or a complete realization of the environmental monitor).

---

# Learning Objectives

By the end of the program students will be able to:

- Explain the difference between AI assistants, agents, and autonomous workflows.
- Design and build simple agentic engineering harnesses.
- Use AI to accelerate electronics design and documentation.
- Use AI-assisted CAD workflows for mechanical design.
- Integrate simulation tools into AI development loops.
- Generate and validate technical documentation.
- Build reproducible engineering workflows using containerized environments.
- Apply AI while maintaining engineering rigor and professional judgment.
- Complete a prototype invention using an AI-assisted product development pipeline.

# Capstone Philosophy

Students will learn that modern engineering is no longer:

Engineer → Tool → Product

Instead, it is becoming:

Engineer → Agentic Workflow → Engineering Tools → Validation → Product

The engineer remains responsible for judgment, creativity, safety, and validation while AI accelerates research, design, documentation, and iteration.
