# Requirements — Environmental Monitor front-end (Track A)

## Electrical

- Sensor-side signal span: 0–3.3 V
- ADC input window: 0–1.0 V
- Accuracy: ±2% of full scale at DC corners (include resistor tolerance)
- Output impedance: < 10 kΩ
- Resistor series: E96

## Deliverables

- design.json with topology and resistor values
- bom.csv
- validation_report.md
- audit_log.jsonl with tool calls and human review
